# SY0-701 Command Center — Design Direction

## Chosen approach: Security Operations Command Center

### Very Brief Intro
A dark intelligence-board interface for focused Security+ mastery. Every surface should feel like a mission module, evidence panel, or readiness console rather than a generic study dashboard.

### Probability
0.06

## Design Movement
Contemporary security operations center HUD language blended with editorial information design: disciplined dark surfaces, telemetry-inspired status signals, and dossier-like modules.

## Core Principles
1. **Mission-first hierarchy:** every screen answers what to study next.
2. **Evidence over decoration:** status, objective IDs, weighting, and mastery are visible and legible.
3. **Signal discipline:** electric blue means action, teal means verified mastery, amber means review, and rose means a real coverage gap.
4. **Operational texture:** scanlines, grid traces, module labels, and compact monospace metadata reinforce the command-center identity.

## Color Philosophy
Use near-black navy as the secure workspace, electric blue as the primary command color, teal for verified progress, amber for review urgency, and rose only for objective gaps. Domain accents remain limited and subordinate to the signal system.

## Layout Paradigm
Asymmetric command-console composition: a wide mission briefing, compact telemetry strip, and modular domain dossiers. Avoid excessive centered cards; use left-anchored content, clear rails, and evidence-oriented groupings.

## Signature Elements
A shield-grid command badge, scanline/grid overlays, and compact status chips with objective IDs.

## Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like deliberate operator actions: clear next steps, immediate feedback, minimal animation, and no hidden state. Review and objective gaps must be reachable in one action.

## Animation
Use short 160–240ms transitions for tab changes, buttons, and module elevation. Avoid decorative looping motion. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography System
Use a strong display face for mission headlines, a readable sans-serif for explanations, and a monospace face for objective IDs, telemetry labels, ports, and status metadata. Uppercase micro-labels carry tracking and should remain short.

## Brand Essence
The practical SY0-701 command console for learners who want objective coverage, not vague confidence. Personality: precise, tactical, accountable.

## Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and mission-oriented. CTAs name the action and outcome.

- “Close the next objective gap.”
- “Review the evidence. Then answer under pressure.”

## Wordmark & Logo
A shield outline interrupted by a three-row grid, paired with a compact SY0-701 command lockup. The mark should read as a protected exam workspace, not a generic shield icon.

## Signature Brand Color
Electric signal blue: `#2F7BFF`.

## Style Decisions
- Visual direction: dark Security Operations Command Center for SY0-701 exam mastery.
- Every major section should feel like an intelligence panel, status board, or mission module.
- Electric blue is reserved for primary study actions; teal marks mastery; amber marks review urgency; rose marks objective gaps.
- Copy sounds like a concise exam-ops briefing: direct, confident, and specific.
- Objective audit is a first-class navigation destination and separates official coverage from anecdotal Reddit-informed practice.
