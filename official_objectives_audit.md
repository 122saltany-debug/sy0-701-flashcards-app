# SY0-701 Official Objectives Audit

## Authoritative baseline
The app is being compared against CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 V7 objectives. The official objectives document identifies five domains and the following exam-weight distribution:

| Domain | Official weighting |
|---|---:|
| 1.0 General Security Concepts | 12% |
| 2.0 Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations | 22% |
| 3.0 Security Architecture | 18% |
| 4.0 Security Operations | 28% |
| 5.0 Security Program Management and Oversight | 20% |

The official CompTIA certification page also describes SY0-701 as a maximum-90-question exam with multiple-choice and performance-based questions, a 90-minute duration, and a passing score of 750 on the 100–900 scale. These exam facts are included only as planning guidance and are not a prediction of question content.

## Initial gap findings

The current app already covers many high-yield terms, but it presents cards primarily by domain rather than by official task identifier. This makes it difficult to verify whether every objective is represented and can cause overconfidence from a large card count. The most important improvement is an objective-level coverage view that shows each task, its official exam weighting, how many cards map to it, and whether the learner has studied it.

The app should also distinguish official objective coverage from anecdotal Reddit-informed practice. Reddit content must remain labeled as original practice synthesis and not be presented as recalled or predicted exam questions.
